Home Roofing FAQs

How long does a typical roof last?
A typical asphalt shingle roof lasts 15 to 25 years. However, premium architectural shingles can last 30+ years, and metal roofs can easily exceed 50 years depending on local climate conditions.
Can you put a new roof over an existing one?
While legally permissible in some areas to overlay one layer of shingles, we strongly advise against it. A complete tear-off allows us to inspect the underlying decking for rot and ensures a much longer lifespan for the new materials.
What are the signs I need a roof replacement?
Look for curling, buckling, or missing shingles. Excessive shingle granules in your gutters, sagging roof decks, and water stains on your interior ceilings are also major red flags indicating it's time for a replacement.
How much does a new roof cost?
Pricing varies significantly based on square footage, pitch, and materials. We provide 100% free, no-obligation detailed estimates after an on-site inspection so you know exactly what to expect.
Do you offer financing options?
Yes! We partner with leading financial institutions to provide flexible, low-interest payment plans. This allows you to secure your home immediately without depleting your savings.
What type of shingles do you use?
We exclusively use top-tier manufacturer products such as GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed, ensuring maximum durability, superior wind resistance, and excellent warranty coverage.
Commercial

Flat Roof & Waterproofing FAQs

What is flat roof waterproofing?
Flat roof waterproofing involves applying a protective barrier—often a liquid polymer or rubberized membrane—over the structural deck. This creates a seamless, watertight seal to prevent ponding water from leaking into the building.
How often should a commercial roof be inspected?
Industry standards recommend inspecting commercial roofs at least twice a year (typically fall and spring), and immediately following any major weather events like severe storms or hail.
Do you fix ponding water on flat roofs?
Yes. Ponding water (water sitting for more than 48 hours) degrades roofing materials fast. We solve this by adding tapered insulation to improve slopes and installing enhanced drainage systems.
What is a liquid-applied membrane?
It is a monolithic, fully-bonded fluid coating that cures to form a rubber-like waterproof membrane. It's highly effective because it has no seams—the most common failure point in traditional commercial roofing.
Will waterproofing disrupt my business operations?
In most cases, no. Liquid-applied systems and modern restoration techniques have low odor and minimal noise compared to a full structural tear-off, allowing your business to operate normally during application.
How long does a commercial warranty last?
Depending on the materials used (TPO, EPDM, or Liquid Coatings), commercial warranties range from 10 to 25 years. We also offer extended workmanship warranties on all our commercial installations.
Terminology

Quick Roofing Glossary

Understand the technical terms our contractors use when explaining your project.

Flashing

Metal strips used to seal roof intersections and edges to prevent water leaks around chimneys, vents, and valleys.

Underlayment

A secondary waterproofing layer (often felt or synthetic) installed directly on the roof deck beneath the shingles.

Pitch

The steepness or slope of a roof, calculated by the number of inches it rises vertically for every 12 inches it extends horizontally.

DIY Tips

Quarterly Maintenance Checklist

Extend the lifespan of your roof by performing these basic checks every season.

Clear Debris

Remove leaves and branches from valleys to prevent moisture trapping.

Clean Gutters

Ensure downspouts are flowing freely to prevent water backup under shingles.

Inspect Flashing

Visually check the caulking and metal around chimneys and vent pipes.

Trim Branches

Cut back overhanging tree limbs to prevent physical scraping damage.
